Privacy policy

Updated October 27, 2026. This policy covers Paw Patrol: Sky Surprises, published by Surprize Game.

Information stored on your device

Best scores, character selection, language and audio preferences stay on your device. The game has no account registration and does not ask for a name, date of birth, email, photos, microphone, contacts or precise location. Scores are not uploaded to an online leaderboard. Clear the app's data in your device settings to remove local records.

Advertising and third-party services

The game uses Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform. Advertising services may process IP addresses, device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostics for advertising delivery and measurement, security and fraud prevention. Advertising identifier handling depends on the platform, child-directed settings and available permissions. The game does not request precise device location. See Google's Privacy Policy and advertising information for Google's data handling and retention practices.

Children and families

The game is designed for children and families. Ad requests are marked as child-directed and restricted to a G content rating. Personalized advertising to children is not intended. There is no chat, user content sharing or social sign-in. Parents can contact support about data or inappropriate advertising. Please do not include a child's personal details in support messages.

Choices and deletion requests

Where required, advertising privacy choices are available in Settings. If you contact support, your message and contact details are used to respond. You can request deletion of support correspondence through the same email address. Use Google's privacy tools for data held by the ad provider. The game developer does not have direct access to the provider's device records.
